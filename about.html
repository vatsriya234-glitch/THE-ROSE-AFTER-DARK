About Riley Rose

Riley Rose is the pen name behind The Rose After Dark, a collection of stories shaped by mystery, emotion, secrets, and complicated relationships.

Her stories explore the moments people hide, the questions nobody asks, and the secrets that refuse to stay buried.

The First Rose is the beginning of her journey into the darker side of storytelling — where every beautiful thing may have a secret behind it.
