A RILEY ROSE STORY

October 14

Some dates are remembered because something happened. Others because someone disappeared.

Riley read the words again.

The first lie was mine.

Her mother's handwriting was unmistakable. Riley had seen it thousands of times. But she had never seen those words.

She opened the old notebook again and searched through every remaining page.

Nothing.

No explanation. No names. No mention of Elias.

Only one page had a faint mark where something had been erased.

Riley held the page beneath her desk lamp.

A few letters were still visible.

"October 14 — Vale House — 11:40 PM"

Riley's heart began to race.

Vale House.

The photograph. The rose. Elias. Noah.

Everything led back to the same night.

She grabbed her jacket.

Then her phone buzzed.

Unknown number.

Riley opened the message.

"You finally found the date."

A second message appeared.

"Now find out what your mother did at 11:40."

Riley stared at the screen.

Someone knew what she had found.

Someone was watching.

She immediately thought of Noah.

Then she remembered the warning.

"Don't trust the boy."

Riley called Maya instead.

Maya answered almost immediately.

"Riley?"

"I know what happened on October 14."

There was silence on the other end.

"What happened?"

"I don't know yet."

Riley looked at the clock.

11:17 PM.

"But I'm going to find out."

Twenty-three minutes later, Riley stood outside Vale House.

The building looked exactly like it had in the photograph.

Dark windows. Iron gates. Vines climbing the walls.

Maya stood beside her, staring at the entrance.

"This is a terrible idea," Maya whispered.

"I know."

"Good. At least we're aware."

Riley almost smiled.

Then the clock on the old building struck 11:40.

Somewhere inside Vale House, a light turned on.

Riley froze.

Someone was inside.

A shadow moved behind the window.

Then the front door slowly opened.

Riley stepped forward.

And someone whispered her name from the darkness.

"Riley Rose."

She knew that voice.

It wasn't Noah.

It wasn't Maya.

It was the voice from her mother's old recordings.

The voice Riley had heard only once.

The voice of Elias Vale.
