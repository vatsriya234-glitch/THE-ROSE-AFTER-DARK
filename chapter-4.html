THE FIRST ROSE

The Boy Who Knows

Sometimes the person closest to the truth is the one hiding it.

CHAPTER FOUR

Riley couldn't stop looking at the photograph.

Not the photograph of her mother and Elias.

The second one.

The one with the face that hadn't been scratched away.

She knew that face.

She just couldn't remember from where.

❦

Noah was still standing across the room.

Riley looked at him.

"You said you knew there was a connection."

Noah nodded.

"I lied."

Riley's expression hardened.

"About what?"

"About how much I knew."

Silence filled the room.

Maya looked between them.

"Maybe someone should start explaining."

Noah finally sat down.

"My father knew Elias Vale."

Riley stared at him.

"How?"

"They worked together."

"Doing what?"

Noah shook his head.

"I don't know everything."

Riley almost laughed.

"You always say that."

Noah looked at her.

"Because it's true."

❦

Noah told them that Elias had disappeared years ago.

His father had never explained exactly what happened.

But after Elias vanished, he became obsessed with finding out where he had gone.

Then, years later, Noah found something in his father's study.

An old photograph.

The same photograph Riley now held.

Except Noah's copy had been different.

Someone had removed the photograph from the frame.

Someone had written a name on the back.

Rose.

Riley's heart sank.

"My mother?"

Noah nodded.

"I didn't know her name at first."

"Then how did you find me?"

Noah looked down.

"My father told me."

❦

Riley felt something inside her crack.

"So you knew who I was before we met."

Noah didn't deny it.

"Yes."

"And you still became my friend?"

Noah was quiet.

"At first, I came looking for answers."

Riley's eyes narrowed.

"And later?"

He looked at her.

"Later, you became more important than the answers."

Riley didn't know what to say.

She wanted to believe him.

But the photograph in her hand reminded her that trust was exactly what she couldn't afford right now.

❦

Maya broke the silence.

"What did your father tell you about Riley's mother?"

Noah hesitated.

"He said there were things she knew."

"What things?"

"I don't know."

Riley looked at him.

"You're lying again."

Noah's jaw tightened.

"I'm not."

"Then tell me everything."

Noah stood.

"I can't."

"Why?"

He looked toward the window.

"Because my father warned me."

Riley froze.

"Warned you about what?"

Noah turned back toward her.

"About you."

❦

Riley couldn't move.

"What did he say?"

Noah's voice became quieter.

"He told me that if Riley Rose ever came looking for the truth, I shouldn't let her find it."

The room suddenly felt smaller.

Riley looked at the photograph.

Then at Noah.

"Why?"

Noah didn't answer.

He simply said,

"Because the truth destroyed your mother."

❦

Noah left shortly afterward.

Maya stayed.

Riley sat quietly at her desk.

The photograph was still in front of her.

Her mother's face looked almost unfamiliar now.

As if every answer Riley found only created another question.

Maya noticed something on the floor.

"Riley."

Riley looked down.

A folded piece of paper had fallen from the second photograph.

Riley picked it up.

There was a single sentence written across it.

"Ask Noah what happened on October 14."

❦

Riley stared at the message.

Then she turned the paper over.

There was something else written there.

A name.

Her mother's name.

Beneath it was another name.

Thomas Gray.

Noah's father.

Riley slowly folded the paper.

For the first time, she understood something.

Noah hadn't simply found her by accident.

Someone had sent him looking for her.

❦

And now Riley had to decide whether Noah was protecting her...

or protecting the secret.
