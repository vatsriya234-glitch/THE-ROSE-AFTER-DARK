The First Rose

Some stories begin with a beautiful lie.
Some secrets are better left buried.

The night was quieter than usual.

Riley Rose had always believed that some memories were meant to fade with time. She was wrong.

It began with a rose.

Not the kind given in daylight, wrapped carefully in paper and tied with a ribbon. This one appeared after midnight, waiting silently where no one should have been.

There was no name attached to it. No explanation. Only a small note.

"Some truths are beautiful until you discover why they were hidden."

Riley stared at the words for a long moment. Then she heard a knock at the door.

And suddenly, the past wasn't buried anymore.
